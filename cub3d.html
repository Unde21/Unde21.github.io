The game

A Wolfenstein-style raycaster turned into a wizard tournament: pick your coalition, explore the maze, open doors with Lumos, travel through the fireplace network and fight spiders, birds and wizards. Two-person project.

Under the hood

Rendering is split across 16 threads (sky, floor, rays, display) synchronised by barriers. In the browser, each pthread becomes a Web Worker sharing the program’s memory. Sounds, originally played with fork and cvlc, go through the browser’s audio.
