The game

Goku collects every crystal ball on the map, then reaches his spaceship to escape. Cell patrols and fires beams; hit him five times to take him down. The step counter is shown in the bottom left corner.

Under the hood

The C code from the repository is compiled to WebAssembly as is. Only the MiniLibX changes: a reimplementation that draws to a canvas and takes the browser’s keyboard input. Maps and XPM sprites are read from a small in-memory file system.
